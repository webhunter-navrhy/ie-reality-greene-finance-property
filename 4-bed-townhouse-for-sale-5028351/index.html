Description

Extending to approximately 153 sq. m, this impressive own-door property offers bright, generously proportioned accommodation arranged over two levels. The lower floor features a superb open-plan kitchen, dining and living area, creating a welcoming space for everyday living and entertaining. Two double bedrooms, a main bathroom and a convenient utility/linen cupboard complete this level.

Upstairs are two exceptionally spacious bedrooms and an en-suite bathroom. The generous dimensions provide excellent flexibility, with ample room to incorporate a home office, dressing area, exercise space or additional relaxation area.

Presented in walk-in condition, the property offers an excellent opportunity for purchasers seeking a permanent home, coastal retreat or investment property in one of County Louth`s most desirable locations.

Harbour Cottages is a well-established residential development located on the South Pier of Carlingford Lough with beautiful waterside walks close by. Carlingford village is renowned for its medieval charm, restaurants, cafés, traditional pubs, hill walk, golf and wide range of other outdoor activities.

Viewing is highly recommended to fully appreciate the exceptional space, versatility and prime location this outstanding property has to offer.
